SAFETY

Side effects, serious risks, and who should not take a GLP-1.

COMMON, AND USUALLY TEMPORARY

Nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea, in 15-20% of patients during the initial phase. These typically resolve within 2-4 weeks as the dose is titrated.

NOT A CANDIDATE IF ANY OF THESE APPLY
  • A personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma
  • Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2
  • Pregnancy, or planning a pregnancy
  • Severe renal impairment
  • An active eating disorder
SERIOUS BUT RARE

Pancreatitis.

Uncommon, but it is the reason we ask about gallbladder disease, triglycerides and alcohol intake before prescribing, and the reason you leave your first visit knowing exactly which symptoms mean stop the injection and call us.

GLP-1 medications have an established safety profile when prescribed and monitored. That second half is the part mail-order skips.
